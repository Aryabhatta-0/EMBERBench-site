# Chart preferences

Read `PRODUCT.md` and `DESIGN.md` before interface changes. The user's chart preferences are persistent:

- Use vertical bar charts, including on phones. Do not switch to horizontal bars to fit a narrow screen.
- Order each displayed series highest to lowest by its own value. Put missing values last. Keep labels explaining when lower values are better.
- When a chart is crowded, enlarge its columns and labels through zoom or continue in readable vertical bands. Preserve every matching model and avoid sideways page scrolling.
- Keep published scores and scientific ranks unchanged. Descending visual order does not redefine official tie-breaks.
