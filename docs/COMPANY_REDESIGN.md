# Anantam — whole-experience rebuild

The user rejected the sparse company hero and concealed accordion as an insufficient change. Those were implementation choices, not approved requirements. This revision replaces that presentation and extends the interaction through worlds, results, model evidence and methodology.

## Visible changes

- A compact, centered Anantam identity brings EMBER into the first viewport’s edge.
- The contribution is visible in reading order: a substantial evacuation stage, two published outcome comparisons, and a connected explanation. There are no hidden primary product panes.
- The stage has three worlds, two routing rules, Play/Pause, Restart and a scrubber. Reference decisions, fire, people and counts change together. Playback is paused by default.
- Outcome cards reorganize exact person-count pictographs on hover; a button provides touch/keyboard access. Counts and labels remain visible at rest.
- All ten public scenario layouts appear on their destination pages. Three support the reference examples; the others remain honestly labeled initial states. Phone choices precede the map and playback sits beside it.
- Scenario → selected model → scenario links preserve context. The scenario’s own action reveals results in place instead of reloading itself.
- Methodology uses a connected observation–decision–simulation loop followed by verification and scoring. Profiles and comparisons retain useful charts while shedding oversized enclosing panels.

The site remains light only. Anantam is an experimental AI company and EMBERBenchmark is its sole current contribution. There are no invented future products, customer claims or achievements.

## Reference methods applied

[Sarvam](https://www.sarvam.ai/) keeps product selection, a substantial example and its next action together. Its API selector changes adjacent code; its product pages expose useful demonstrations before asking for commitment. [Gnani](https://www.gnani.ai/) connects model-specific motion, capability, evidence and action. Its enterprise tabs preserve context while switching the explanation.

Anantam applies those methods to a playable public environment, visible outcomes and contextual evidence. It does not copy their artwork, gradients, products, claims or page text. See [the primary-source research](../design/research.md) and [the visitor audit](../design/audit.md).

## Scientific boundary

The six examples are independently generated **hand-coded reference runs**, not the published AI-model runs. They use the official EMBERBench simulator at commit `f0ba4774cecae7c3dad83ebaee4f2e3e8e7d41c8` and each scenario’s default seed. Upstream `export_animation` and `verify_animation` authenticated every complete run. Full RunRecords, record digests, animation provenance and Apache-2.0 attribution ship locally. `tools/build_demo_assets.py` reproduces them with an upstream checkout and Python3.12+.

Published scores, model identities and counts still come exclusively from the unchanged `site/data/results.js`. People counts sum across worlds; the score also weighs execution quality and balances scenario families. More evacuations can therefore accompany a lower score. The one-trial, public-development and descriptive-comparison limits remain visible.

## Verification and limits

Runtime checks cover replay endpoints against the authenticated exports, record-file hashes, pause/restart/scrubbing, different routing rules, no stale source links, explicit touch grouping, intermediate hover movement, context-preserving navigation, native keyboard controls, loading failures, no-GSAP recovery, reduced motion and light appearance. All analytical/data checks remain in the suite.

Rendered review covers current desktop and phone home, world, methodology, profile and comparison captures. Browser checks are Chromium-based; physical phones, Safari/WebKit, Firefox and the protected deployment were not runtime-verified. This is an engineering and heuristic review, not a study with recruited participants. Test counts and final CI state are recorded in the delivered report after verification.
