# Privacy policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Anantam is the name used for this independent AI research project. It is not currently a registered company. This policy covers anantam-labs.vercel.app and the EMBERBench information published on it. For privacy requests, email [arsathvik48@gmail.com](mailto:arsathvik48@gmail.com).

## What happens when you visit

The website publishes research, documentation and public-development benchmark results. It has no account registration, payment checkout, submission form, advertising, analytics tracker or embedded social-media feed. Fonts, images, scripts and the published results are served from the website's own origin.

Our hosting provider, Vercel, receives the network information needed to deliver and protect the site. This can include your IP address, requested address, browser information, timestamps and security diagnostics. Hosting and security data may be processed by Vercel and its service providers under their policies. See [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice).

## Search, filters and browser storage

Documentation search runs in your browser against a public search index. Model filters also run in your browser. Search terms, filters and selected models can appear in page addresses so you can revisit or share a view. When you open or reload one of those addresses, its query string may reach our hosting provider. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information in search fields or URLs.

Comparison selections are kept only for the current visit unless you choose to remember them on your device. The [Cookies and preferences](cookies.html) page explains the storage keys, lets you control that choice and lets you clear this site's saved preferences. The website works when optional storage is off or unavailable.

## If you contact us

We receive the email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to answer your enquiry, handle a privacy or security request and keep records where needed to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute. Please send only what is necessary; do not email passwords, API keys, identity documents or private benchmark submissions.

Email delivery also involves your email provider and Gmail, the provider of our current contact mailbox. Their own terms and privacy practices apply. We do not sell enquiry data or use it for advertising.

## Why information is used and shared

We use information to deliver the site, maintain security, answer communications and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell or rent personal information, share it for behavioural advertising or build visitor advertising profiles. We may disclose information to service providers needed for those purposes, when legally required, or to protect people and the service. Opening GitHub, provider websites or other external links takes you to services with their own policies.

Where a law requires a legal basis, the basis depends on the activity and the law that applies: providing a service you requested, legitimate interests in operating and securing a public research website where recognised, your choice for optional device storage, or compliance with a legal obligation. This policy does not treat simply browsing as consent to unrelated processing.

## Retention, location and security

You control optional browser storage; it remains until you clear it, turn it off, or your browser removes it. We keep correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, security, legal obligations or a dispute, then delete or anonymise it. Hosting-provider retention follows the provider's applicable terms and settings; we do not promise an unverified fixed log-retention period.

Hosting and email providers may process information internationally. We use their available security and contractual safeguards, but cannot guarantee that internet transmissions or storage are completely secure.

## Your choices and requests

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, a copy of information, restriction or objection, or to withdraw a choice previously given. Email the contact above with enough context to identify the relevant interaction. We may need proportionate verification before releasing personal information. We respond within applicable legal time limits. You may also contact your local data-protection authority where available. Mandatory rights are not limited by this policy.

This is a public research website, not a service directed at children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe a child sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

## Changes

We update this page when the website or its data practices change and show the effective date above. Material changes requiring a new choice will be presented before the relevant optional processing starts.
