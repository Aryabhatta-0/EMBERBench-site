# Visitor audit and resolution

Target: local Anantam site on port 8927. Goal: understand the benchmark and reach relevant evidence without decoding the interface first.

| Priority | Confirmed friction | Change | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High | Default mixed cohort obscures AI versus hand-coded reference agents. | Default to AI decision models; explicit cohort filter and explanation; references remain comparable. | Real-data ordering and reference-filter journeys. |
| High | Scores appear before useful meaning. | Visible scale, composite-score explanation, selected model's exact people outcomes and scenario links. | Source-value checks, real visitor clicks, desktop/phone captures. |
| High | Table requires scanning many numbers to discover a difference. | Fixed-scale result bars; adjacent detail and immediate comparison; exact table remains available. | Metric changes, scale checks, model-identity and reload tests. |
| High | Initial profile and comparison views emphasize seven technical factors. | Outcomes and difficult scenarios lead; component evidence moves behind named disclosures. | Profile/comparison navigation and retained source evidence tests. |
| Medium | Preview motion alone offers little exploration. | Two source-backed initial worlds; matched scenario actions; actual score bars opening selected results. | Geometry counts, scenario identity and pointer/keyboard/touch checks. |
| Medium | Phone selection can separate an item from its explanation. | Place the inspector immediately below its selected row. | 320px and 390px DOM/order and overflow checks. |
| Medium | Existing profile score styles can form a clipped horizontal line. | Explicit score layout, bounded type and responsive wrapping. | Profile captures and all-route width checks. |

Axe scans cover selected WCAG A/AA rules. Keyboard, touch-equivalent operation, reduced motion and visible reading journeys were checked separately. These are engineering and heuristic checks, not a claim of a full screen-reader audit or measured improvement with recruited participants.
