Scoring sensitivity study: full output (scoring version 1.0)

This file is the unedited output of python tools/scoring_sensitivity.py for scoring version 1.0 (progress lines removed; headings demoted by one level). The analysis is in benchmark-methodology.md, sections 5.12 and 6. Unless a table says otherwise, scores are family-balanced means over the two study suites: the public suite (seeds 0, 1, 2) and a generated stochastic study suite (16 scenarios, seeds 0, 1, 2). The held-out suite is a second generated suite from the same config and a fresh pre-registered generator seed (20261021, seeds 0, 1, 2); it was not used to choose anything. The adversarial policies, including adv-block-scrub, are study instruments, not baselines. F-official (and G5-official) is the official formula; the other columns are the rejected designs of benchmark-methodology.md, sections 5.9 and 5.12. Episodes where nobody can escape (not_applicable) are excluded from every mean, as in the score.
