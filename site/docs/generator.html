PR #33 (evaluation-suite-design.md) froze the V1 evaluation-suite design: 10 primary families x 16 scenarios (Easy 2, Moderate 4, Hard 6, Stress 4), 12 deterministic and 4 stochastic scenarios per family, 3 unique fire worlds per stochastic scenario: 160 scenarios and 240 unique worlds. It also showed a gap: the generator labelled only three primary families and called every multi-exit scenario split flow (section 6 there). Phase 11 closes the gap without changing any of those numbers and without changing the simulator or the score. The PR #33 rules moved, unchanged, from the research tool into the library, and the research tool now imports them.

1. Who computes what

Part Side Seed or world needed? Private during a live evaluation?
Structural proxies (measure_proxies, profile_structure) generator no (seed-free, scenario only) the values of hidden scenarios are
Structural family tags (structural_tags): 8 primary families and both secondary tags generator no the tags of hidden scenarios are
Generation constraints and recipes generator no the recipes and rules are public; the generator seeds are private
Fire evidence (fire_evidence): threatened share, (recoverable) invalidation share evaluator yes: one fire trajectory per world yes
Fire-dependent tags (threatened_nearest_exit, route_invalidation_replanning) evaluator yes: deterministic fire uses its one trajectory; stochastic fire uses the mean over the scenario's selected unique worlds yes
Calibration (panel scores, M*, M_cap, capacity ceiling), admission, band evaluator yes: every unique world yes

The generator never labels difficulty, never sees an evaluation seed and never reads a model. A stochastic scenario's family never depends on a model: its fire-dependent tags are measured on exactly the unique worlds that build_unique_world_manifest will schedule for it (the same candidate-seed list and the same walk, world_seeds_for), and the evidence is frozen into the freeze record.

2. The ten family rules (family rules 1.0, the exact PR #33 thresholds)

Family Rule Side
single_exit_low_density one exit and crowd density < 0.15 generator
single_exit_high_density one exit and crowd density >= 0.15 generator
narrow_corridor_bottleneck passage_width <= 1 and bottleneck_severity = N / min_cut >= 4 generator
split_flow_across_exits several exits, split_pressure > 1 and N >= 20 generator
crowd_100 N >= 100 generator
maze_obstacle_routing obstacle density >= 0.25 or detour ratio >= 1.5 generator
deceptive_nearest_exit several exits and deceptive_share >= 0.25 generator
threatened_nearest_exit several exits and mean threatened share >= 0.2 evaluator (fire)
multiple_ignitions at least 2 ignitions generator
route_invalidation_replanning mean recoverable invalidation share >= 0.2 evaluator (fire)
central_ignition (secondary) ignition centrality <= 0.34 generator
multiple_exits_unequal_distance (secondary) several exits and median exit gap >= 4; also the fallback tag of a multi-exit scenario with no other tag generator

Secondary tags never become a primary family: primary_candidates drops them and the design has no cell for them. family_rules() returns this table in canonical form; it is part of the frozen rule set and its digest.

Split flow (the PR #33 fix). Several exits are not enough. split_pressure is the busiest route-nearest exit's load divided by 1 plus the median extra route its people would walk to their second-nearest exit. A low-pressure multi-exit scenario (people already spread over far-apart exits) is not split flow; a scenario with 20 or more people crowding one exit while another is almost as close is. The split-flow-medium recipes produce split flow at 24-60 people, so the family is not only a crowd_100 side effect.

Deceptive nearest exit. deceptive_share is the share of people whose Manhattan-nearest exit is a dead end or at least 2 steps longer by route than their route-nearest exit. It is seed-free. Config 1.1 can constrain it.

Narrow corridor. A door is not a bottleneck by itself: the passage must be 1 cell wide for some person and the crowd must need at least 4 steps to drain through the narrowest cut (N / min_cut >= 4). Two people behind a 1-cell door are not a bottleneck; eight people behind a 3-cell door are not either.

3. Generator config 1.1

Config 1.1 adds three optional constraint ranges: split_pressure, deceptive_share and bottleneck_severity. They aim a recipe at one structural family by rejection sampling. Details and the new proxies: procedural-generation.md, section 6. The generator stream label contains GENERATOR_VERSION, so generator 1.1.0 produces different scenarios than 1.0.0 for the same seed and config (the sampling code itself did not change). The public suite does not come from the generator and its hash is unchanged.

The first ten are the PR #33 development recipes, the next four its gap-probe recipes (crowded maze; stochastic maze, dense single exit and crowd), the rest are new targeted recipes. Threatened exit and route invalidation cannot be constrained without a fire trajectory: their recipes raise the rate (exit close to an ignition; two doors per partition so a route can be cut and still be recovered) and evaluator-side tagging plus rejection does the rest. The initial plan totals 4,000 candidates (V1_INITIAL_PLAN). The top-up table V1_TOP_UP maps each family and fire model to one dedicated recipe.

5. Pipeline (compose_pool, compose_suite, freeze_composed_suite)

  1. Generate every planned recipe batch (at most 200 candidates per batch) with generate_suite. The generator seed of a batch is SHA-256("emberbench/candidate-pool/1.0/<recipe>/<version>/<batch>/" || pool_seed); only its commitment is recorded. Drop duplicate content hashes and every excluded hash (the public suite, earlier rounds).
  2. Unique worlds. For each candidate, the indices of the private evaluation seeds that build_unique_world_manifest will schedule: the first seed for deterministic fire, the first 3 seeds with pairwise-different environment_world_digest for stochastic fire. A stochastic candidate with fewer distinct worlds is dropped as insufficient_worlds.
  3. Frozen calibration panel on every unique world (measure_world): the official EMBER Score of each panel member (score_evidence on episode_evidence, the same references shared as in score_results), M*, M_cap, the capacity ceiling and the fire evidence.
  4. Admission (calibrate, in order): not applicable, capacity artifact (mean capacity ceiling < 50, before any other rule), saturated, floor, no separation, admitted.
  5. Difficulty band on B, the best panel mean: Easy >= 80, Moderate >= 30, Hard >= 10, Stress below.
  6. Family tags: family_tags(profile, [fire evidence per world]).
  7. Quotas (select_suite, selection rules 1.0): families scarcest first (fewest eligible candidates, ties by name); in a family, bands Easy..Stress and the stochastic cell before the deterministic one; in a cell, candidates ordered by SHA-256("emberbench/suite-selection/" + key + "/" + hash). Each candidate fills one cell, and that cell's family becomes its one primary family. A short cell is reported, never filled from another band or fire model.
  8. Top-up: while a cell is short and the pool is below 10,000 candidates, one round generates one 200-candidate batch of the V1_TOP_UP recipe for each short cell's family and fire model, measures it and fills only the short cells (the earlier selection stays fixed). The stopping rule reads cell counts only.
  9. Relabel and freeze: neutral IDs gen-NNNN in the order of SHA-256("emberbench/suite-order/" + key + "/" + hash); each relabeled scenario is checked to keep every fire trajectory; then <dir>/suite/ (a normal suite) and <dir>/suite-freeze.json are written once and loaded back verified. schedule_composed_worlds builds the evaluation manifest with build_unique_world_manifest and checks every world digest against the freeze record.

The result is a pure function of the candidate content, the frozen rules, the private pool seed, evaluation seeds and selection key, and the quotas. Worker count, candidate order, clock and model identity do not change it (tested).

6. Suite freeze record (suite-freeze.json, version 1.0)

Key Content
suite suite ID, version, hash
rule_set, rule_set_digest every version (generator, generator config, generation manifest, recipe catalog, family rules, selection rules, calibration panel, difficulty rules, admission rules, simulator, observation, scenario schema, scoring, environment world), the family rules, the calibration rules, the selection rules with the top-up table, the design with every cell quota, every recipe with its config and config hash
commitments SHA-256 commitments to the pool seed, the evaluation seed list and the selection key
batches per batch: recipe, version, batch index and ID, candidates, config hash, generator-seed commitment, generation hash
pool candidates per recipe, admission outcomes, eligible candidates per cell, dropped duplicates
selected per scenario: neutral ID, final hash, candidate hash, origin (recipe, batch, ordinal), primary family, band, fire model, all tags, structural tags, proxies, calibration, and per world the seed index, environment_world_digest, M*, M_cap, capacity ceiling, fire evidence and panel scores
shortfall every short cell

It holds no seed and no key. It is still private while the suite is live: it identifies the scenarios. SuiteFreezeRecord.public_commitment() (suite hash, freeze-record digest, rule-set digest, commitments, cell counts, shortfall size) is what may be published before the evaluation. The leak guard rejects suite-freeze.json anywhere in the repository or a distribution.

7. Generator coverage validation

tools/validate_generator_coverage.py run --out <dir outside the repo> runs the whole pipeline on a development pool built from public seed labels (emberbench/generator-coverage/development/). It reports candidates per recipe, admission outcomes, eligible candidates per family, band and fire model, the rarest cells, the selection and its shortfall, keyed-subsample pool sizes, the overlap audit, fire-dependent tag counts and runtime, then freezes a development suite and checks its 240-world schedule. This pool is not, and must never be called, a hidden suite. Results: generator-coverage-results.md.
