Contact Anantam

Research enquiries, corrections and website support

Email arsathvik48@gmail.com. This is the current public contact for Anantam, an independent research initiative that is not yet a registered company. We do not publish a postal address, phone number or office location.

Research and website questions

For a research question or correction, include the page address, the model or metric involved, and the relevant public source. For a website problem, include the browser, device and steps that led to it. Please share only information necessary to explain the issue. This mailbox is not an official evaluation-submission service; do not send private model credentials, confidential test suites or API keys.

Public code issues can also be reported in the website repository or benchmark repository. Those are public channels. Keep personal information and security-sensitive details out of public issues.

Privacy and accessibility

Use the same email for a privacy request, a question about cookies and preferences, or an accessibility barrier. Include enough context to locate the relevant interaction without sending identity documents or unrelated personal information. The privacy policy explains how correspondence and hosting information are handled.

Security reports

Email security-sensitive findings privately to the contact above rather than posting them in a public issue. Include the affected URL and a minimal explanation. Do not access other people's information, disrupt the site or continue beyond what is necessary to describe a concern. This contact route does not grant permission to test systems or promise a bounty. A machine-readable contact is available in security.txt.

We do not promise a fixed response time or round-the-clock support. If there is an actual fire or other emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service; this research website and mailbox are not emergency channels.
