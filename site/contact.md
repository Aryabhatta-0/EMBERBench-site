# Contact Anantam

Research enquiries, corrections and website support

Email [arsathvik48@gmail.com](mailto:arsathvik48@gmail.com). This is the current public contact for Anantam, an independent research initiative that is not yet a registered company. We do not publish a postal address, phone number or office location.

## Research and website questions

For a research question or correction, include the page address, the model or metric involved, and the relevant public source. For a website problem, include the browser, device and steps that led to it. Please share only information necessary to explain the issue. This mailbox is not an official evaluation-submission service; do not send private model credentials, confidential test suites or API keys.

Public code issues can also be reported in the [website repository](https://github.com/Aryabhatta-0/EMBERBench-site/issues) or [benchmark repository](https://github.com/Aryabhatta-0/EMBERBench/issues). Those are public channels. Keep personal information and security-sensitive details out of public issues.

## Privacy and accessibility

Use the same email for a privacy request, a question about [cookies and preferences](cookies.html), or an accessibility barrier. Include enough context to locate the relevant interaction without sending identity documents or unrelated personal information. The [privacy policy](privacy.html) explains how correspondence and hosting information are handled.

## Security reports

Email security-sensitive findings privately to the contact above rather than posting them in a public issue. Include the affected URL and a minimal explanation. Do not access other people's information, disrupt the site or continue beyond what is necessary to describe a concern. This contact route does not grant permission to test systems or promise a bounty. A machine-readable contact is available in [security.txt](.well-known/security.txt).

We do not promise a fixed response time or round-the-clock support. If there is an actual fire or other emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service; this research website and mailbox are not emergency channels.
