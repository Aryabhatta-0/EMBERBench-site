Research scope

EMBERBench studies decision-making in simulated fire evacuations. The displayed results describe a public-development snapshot, which models may train or tune against. They are reproducible development evidence, not contamination-resistant official hidden-test results. The published snapshot has one trial per model. An EMBER score is a composite measure, not a survival percentage or a guarantee of real-world performance.

The website is for research and information. It is not emergency advice, a fire-safety system, professional advice or a substitute for emergency services and qualified safety professionals. Do not use its charts or simulations to direct an actual evacuation. Read the methodology and evaluation scope when interpreting or citing results.
