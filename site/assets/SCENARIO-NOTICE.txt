site/data/worlds.js contains only initial geometry.
assets/demos contains six independently generated illustrative reference runs, their complete RunRecords, and authenticated animation exports. They use static-shortest-path-v1 and fire-aware-shortest-path-v1 on corridor-bottleneck, unequal-exits and threatened-exit scenarios at each scenario's default seed.
Every export passed upstream export_animation and verify_animation, including full simulator replay authentication. Source commit and record digests are in site/data/demo-index.js and the animation documents.
These examples are not the published AI-model runs and are not used to calculate any displayed benchmark score. Published results remain exclusively in site/data/results.js.
Reproduce with Python 3.12+: python tools/build_demo_assets.py PATH_TO_EMBERBENCH_CHECKOUT
